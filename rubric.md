# Self-assessment rubric — 0–3, same scale all 12 weeks

Score each week's artefact against these anchors. Score honestly: calibration is
part of the skill. One sentence of justification is required with every score.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| **0 — absent** | Artefact not produced. Record it as 0, not blank. |
| **1 — present** | Artefact exists but is generic; it could have been written without doing the week's reading. |
| **2 — applied** | Uses the week's framework correctly on the lab data; concrete numbers, named columns, a query or model someone else could run. |
| **3 — critiqued** | Applied AND self-critiqued: names a weakness in the measure, a false positive it will raise, a way the number could be gamed, or the counter-measure it needs. |

The 3 anchor is the normal outcome of a good week, not extra credit. Weeks 5, 7
and 10 are explicitly about the weaknesses of measures.

Gate thresholds (weeks 4, 8, 12): pass at average ≥ 2 across the gate's pieces,
with at least one 3 required at the week 12 capstone.
